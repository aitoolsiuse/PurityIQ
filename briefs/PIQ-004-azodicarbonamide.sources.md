Notes
- eCFR and Federal Register web pages redirect to the unverified host "unblock.federalregister.gov" and were not followed. CFR text is from govinfo.gov (2024 edition, title 21 vol 3); the Federal Register notice was read from govinfo.gov (same document, FR Doc 2026-09508). Currency check for 172.806: the May 2026 notice restates the same limits, and FDA's review-list page (updated 29 July 2026) shows no regulatory action. The 2025 and 2026 CFR editions were not fetched.
- The FDA FAQ page carries "Content current as of: 01/04/2018". Its statements on SEM, tumors and consumer diets are FDA's 2018 wording; FDA's 2026 reassessment could change them. The FAQ also says FDA "considers ADA a safe food additive" under the regulation (banned word, verbatim, not reused on slides).
- EU claim rests on the Annex II list as adopted in 2011. Consolidated current versions of Reg 1333/2008 could not be retrieved (EUR-Lex returned "document does not exist" for guessed consolidation dates), so later amendments adding E 927a were not ruled out by direct reading. The UK GOV.UK list (June 2026) and the Commission comment document support the same conclusion.
- EFSA has not been shown to have issued an opinion on ADA baked into bread. The 2005 EFSA item concerns semicarbazide from lid gaskets. Do not attribute the bread conversion statement to EFSA.
- Australia: no government source read. FSANZ page fetched did not mention ADA and the Food Standards Code Schedule 15 was not confirmed. Not confirmed.
- WHO/JECFA: the 2023 ADI withdrawal is stated on the JECFA database page as a CCFA53 action. Do not describe it as a JECFA finding of harm; it says an ADI is no longer supported because no data were provided for re-evaluation.
- Other states (New York, Pennsylvania bills, other enacted school-meal laws) were not verified from legislature sites and are excluded.
- Brand rule: no product or brand is named in any claim above.
