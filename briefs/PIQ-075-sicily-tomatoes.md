title: Salmonella and Sicilian Tomatoes

## Caption

Germany's Robert Koch Institute reports its largest Salmonella Strathcona outbreak yet: 116 cases in the 2025/26 season, with the strain found in organic cherry tomatoes from Italy and in a Sicilian producer's irrigation water; 39 more cases since June 2026. The strain has caused over 600 cases in 17 European countries since 2011. No US cases are reported. Source: RKI Epidemiologisches Bulletin 39/2026 (Sept 24, 2026).
