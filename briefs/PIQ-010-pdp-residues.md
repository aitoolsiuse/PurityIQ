## Caption

In USDA's 2024 testing, only 5.7% of 704 apple samples had no detectable pesticide residue. None of the 704 exceeded an EPA tolerance, and USDA reports that more than 99 percent of all samples were below EPA tolerances. Detected does not mean over the limit. Sources: USDA AMS Pesticide Data Program Annual Summary, Calendar Year 2024 (December 2025), Appendices H, I and J and the Executive Summary. Weekly breakdown at the link in bio.
