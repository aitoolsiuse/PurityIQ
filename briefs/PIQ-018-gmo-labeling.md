## Caption

The EU requires GMO labeling based on a flat 0.9% content threshold. The US bioengineered food standard exempts a product if the modified genetic material can't be detected in the final product, and allows the disclosure to be a QR code paired with a phone number instead of words on the package. Sources: Regulation (EC) No 1829/2003, Articles 12 and 13; 7 CFR 66.1, 66.5, 66.9 and 66.100-66.106; USDA AMS List of Bioengineered Foods. Weekly breakdown at the link in bio.
