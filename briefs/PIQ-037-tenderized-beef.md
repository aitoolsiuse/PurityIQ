title: Needle-Tenderized Beef Labels

## Caption

Since May 17, 2016, USDA has required raw beef that was mechanically tenderized by needle or blade to say so in the product name and carry cooking instructions. USDA estimates about 10.5 percent of beef products sold are mechanically tenderized. The rule does not cover pork or poultry. Sources: 9 CFR 317.2(e)(3); USDA FSIS final rule, 80 FR 28153 (May 18, 2015).
