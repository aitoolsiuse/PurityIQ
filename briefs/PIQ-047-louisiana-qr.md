title: Louisiana Ingredient QR Codes

## Caption

Louisiana's Act 463 (2025), as amended by Act 735 (2026), will require foods containing any of 43 listed ingredients to carry a QR code linking to an ingredient notice, starting December 31, 2028. From the 2028-29 school year, Louisiana schools can't serve 14 listed ingredients, including aspartame and sucralose. FDA allows aspartame as a sweetener. Sources: Louisiana Act 463 (2025); Act 735 (2026); 21 CFR 172.804.
