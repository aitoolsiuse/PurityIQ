title: Arsenic in Infant Rice Cereal

## Caption

FDA's August 2020 guidance sets an action level of 100 parts per billion for inorganic arsenic in infant rice cereal. FDA testing found 76 percent of samples at or below that level in 2018, up from 36 percent in 2011 to 2013. FDA notes that oat, barley and multigrain iron-fortified cereals can also be a first infant cereal. Sources: FDA, Inorganic Arsenic in Rice Cereals for Infants: Action Level (August 2020); FDA constituent update, August 5, 2020.
