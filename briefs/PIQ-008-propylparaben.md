## Caption

California will bar selling food that contains propylparaben starting January 1, 2027. The EU removed it (E 216) from its list of authorized food preservatives, with a 2008 deadline, while FDA's rules still list it and FDA has it on its chemical review list. Sources: California AB 418 (Chapter 328, Statutes of 2023, codified at section 109025); Directive 2006/52/EC; Regulation (EC) No 1333/2008, consolidated version of 18 August 2026; 21 CFR 184.1670 and 101.22(j); FDA List of Select Chemicals in the Food Supply Under FDA Review.
