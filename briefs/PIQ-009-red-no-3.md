## Caption

FDA ended its Red No. 3 listing for cosmetics in 1990. Its food listing ends on January 15, 2027. FDA says the tumors seen in male rats are of limited relevance to humans, and it acted under a law that applies to additives shown to cause cancer in animals. The EU authorizes the same dye only in certain preserved cherries. Sources: FDA final order, 90 FR 4628 (January 16, 2025); FDA notice, 91 FR 50475 (August 5, 2026); Commission Regulation (EU) No 1129/2011 and Regulation (EC) No 1333/2008; 21 CFR 101.22(k).
