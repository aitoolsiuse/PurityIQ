title: What Strawberry Flavored Means

## Caption

FDA rules: when a food has no strawberries, or too few to give the flavor on its own, the name must say 'strawberry flavored', with 'flavored' at least half the height of the fruit name. Foods with some fruit plus other flavors must say 'with other natural flavor', and any artificial flavor must be named on the front. Source: 21 CFR 101.22(i).
