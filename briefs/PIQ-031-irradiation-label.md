title: The Irradiation Symbol

## Caption

FDA requires irradiated foods to carry the international Radura symbol and the statement 'Treated with radiation' or 'Treated by irradiation.' FDA does not require individual irradiated ingredients in multi-ingredient foods, such as spices, to be labeled. FDA has approved irradiation for foods including beef, pork, poultry, shellfish, fresh produce, shell eggs and spices. Source: FDA, Food Irradiation: What You Need to Know.
