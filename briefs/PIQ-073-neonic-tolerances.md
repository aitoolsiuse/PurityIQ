title: EPA Review of Five Insecticides

## Caption

EPA must decide by April 30, 2027 on a petition to revoke all food tolerances for five neonicotinoid insecticides: acetamiprid, clothianidin, dinotefuran, imidacloprid and thiamethoxam (Federal Register, Aug 20, 2026). The EU lowered its limits for clothianidin and thiamethoxam to the lab detection level, applying to imports from March 7, 2026, to protect pollinators. USDA organic rules bar synthetic substances not on the National List. Sources: 91 FR (Aug 20, 2026); Regulation (EU) 2023/334; 7 CFR 205.105.
