title: Idaho Lab-Grown Meat Labels

## Caption

Since July 1, 2026, Idaho requires cell-cultivated animal protein to be labeled 'lab-grown,' 'cell-cultivated,' or 'cell-cultured' on the front panel, at least as prominent as the product name, and bars cut names like steak or brisket unless USDA authorizes them. Federally, USDA oversees labeling, but its labeling rule has not been proposed. Sources: Idaho S1270 (2026, Ch. 247); FDA, Human Food Made with Cultured Animal Cells; Unified Agenda RIN 0583-AD89.
