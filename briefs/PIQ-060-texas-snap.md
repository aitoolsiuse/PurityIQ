title: Texas SNAP Sweetened Drink Rule

## Caption

Since April 1, 2026, a USDA waiver excludes sweetened drinks and candy from SNAP purchases in Texas. Sweetened drinks are defined as water-based drinks with 5 grams or more of added sugar or any artificial sweetener; drinks with milk, milk substitutes or more than 50 percent juice are excluded from the definition. A federal court blocked similar waivers in five other states (June 2026). Sources: USDA waiver approval and modification (Aug 2025; Feb 25, 2026); 7 CFR 271.2; Aragon v. Rollins order (D.D.C., June 22, 2026).
