title: EU Bans BPA in Food Packaging

## Caption

EU Regulation 2024/3190 bans BPA in food contact materials; single-use packaging made with BPA could only be sold until July 20, 2026, with cans for fruit, vegetables and fish allowed until January 20, 2028. The EU acted after its food agency found a health concern from dietary BPA for all population groups. FDA still allows BPA in approved food packaging uses. Sources: Regulation (EU) 2024/3190; FDA, Bisphenol A (BPA).
