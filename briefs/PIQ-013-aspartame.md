## Caption

In July 2023, one WHO group called aspartame "possibly carcinogenic," and on the same day another WHO group, JECFA, reaffirmed its existing daily limit. Both groups still allow it. They were answering two different questions: can it ever cause cancer, versus what happens at the doses people actually consume. Sources: IARC press release, July 14, 2023; JECFA summary and conclusions, 2023; FDA, Aspartame and Other Sweeteners in Food (updated February 27, 2025); 21 CFR 172.804.
