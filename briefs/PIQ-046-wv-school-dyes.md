title: West Virginia School Dye Ban

## Caption

Since August 1, 2025, West Virginia school meals can't include Red 3, Red 40, Yellow 5, Yellow 6, Blue 1, Blue 2 or Green 3. A federal court in December 2025 blocked the state's separate 2028 statewide ban but not the school-meal rule. FDA still lists Red 40 for coloring foods generally. Sources: West Virginia HB 2354 (2025); S.D. W.Va. order, Dec 23, 2025; 21 CFR 74.340.
