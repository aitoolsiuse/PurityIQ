## Caption

FDA is reassessing azodicarbonamide, a bread and flour additive. It is not on the EU or UK lists of authorized food additives, while the US allows up to 45 parts per million in flour. FDA says it must appear on the ingredient label and is not necessary to make bread. Sources: FDA Request for Information, 91 FR (May 13, 2026), docket FDA-2026-N-4126; 21 CFR 172.806; FDA List of Select Chemicals in the Food Supply Under FDA Review; FDA azodicarbonamide FAQ (2018); Regulation (EU) No 1129/2011, Annex II; UK Food Standards Agency approved additives list (June 2026).
