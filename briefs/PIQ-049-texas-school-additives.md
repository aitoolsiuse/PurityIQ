title: Texas School Meal Additive Ban

## Caption

Texas SB 314 bars 17 named additives from free and reduced-price school meals starting in the 2026-27 school year, including Class III and IV caramel colors and titanium dioxide. FDA allows caramel and titanium dioxide for coloring foods generally. Colors not subject to certification may be listed only as 'Artificial Color' or 'Color Added.' Sources: Texas SB 314 (2025); 21 CFR 73.85; 21 CFR 73.575; 21 CFR 101.22.
