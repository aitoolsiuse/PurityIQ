title: Wheat Flour Is White Flour

## Caption

Under FDA's standards, 'wheat flour' is another name for white flour, and FDA says wheat flour should not be labeled as whole grain. Whole wheat bread must be made with whole wheat flour, with no white flour. Ingredients are listed by weight, so check the first one. Sources: 21 CFR 137.105; 21 CFR 136.180; FDA draft guidance on whole grain label statements (2006); 21 CFR 101.4.
