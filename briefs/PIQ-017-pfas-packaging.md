title: PFAS in Food Packaging

## Caption

Since January 2025, FDA has formally ended authorization for the PFAS grease-proofing chemicals once used in fast food wrappers and microwave popcorn bags. Since August 2026, the EU limits PFAS in food packaging under its own packaging law. Three other PFAS uses remain authorized in US food contact. Sources: FDA, Authorized Uses of PFAS in Current Food Packaging; FDA notice, January 3, 2025; European Commission, Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation (EU) 2025/40.
