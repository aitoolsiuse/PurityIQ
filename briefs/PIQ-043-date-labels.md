title: What Food Date Labels Mean

## Caption

Except for infant formula, federal law doesn't require date labels on food, and FDA supports 'Best if Used By' as the standard quality phrase. California's AB 660 requires 'BEST if Used by' for quality or 'USE by' for safety on foods made on or after July 1, 2026. Sources: FDA, How to Cut Food Waste; 21 CFR 107.20; FDA/USDA date labeling RFI (Dec 3, 2024); California AB 660.
