title: Which Fish FDA Says to Skip

## Caption

FDA's advice about eating fish, for those who are or might become pregnant or are breastfeeding and for children ages 1 to 11, sorts fish by mercury. Choices to avoid: king mackerel, marlin, orange roughy, shark, swordfish, tilefish from the Gulf of Mexico and bigeye tuna. Best choices include salmon, sardines and catfish. Source: FDA, Advice about Eating Fish (revised October 2021).
