title: Hidden Caffeine on Labels

## Caption

FDA says added caffeine must be listed in the ingredients, but the amount is voluntary, and caffeine that comes with an ingredient like chocolate chips won't be listed at all. FDA has no regulation specific to energy drinks, which it says generally contain 54 to 328 mg of caffeine per 16 ounces. Sources: FDA, Spilling the Beans: How Much Caffeine Is Too Much? (updated 8/28/2024); 21 CFR 182.1180.
