title: Arizona's Ultraprocessed Food Rule

## Caption

Beginning in the 2026-27 school year, Arizona schools in federal meal programs can't serve or sell 'ultraprocessed food' during the school day. State law defines it as food with any of 11 listed ingredients. FDA and USDA say there is no single, universally accepted definition. Parents may still pack these foods. Sources: Arizona Laws 2025, Ch. 52 (ARS 15-242.01); FDA/USDA RFI, 90 FR 35305 (July 25, 2025).
