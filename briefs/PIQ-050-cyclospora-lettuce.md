title: Cyclospora and Shredded Lettuce

## Caption

A Cyclospora outbreak linked to shredded iceberg lettuce sickened 12,883 people in 21 states, with 570 hospitalizations and 2 deaths, FDA reports. CDC declared it over on September 11, 2026; FDA's investigation of lettuce from central Mexico continues. FDA says commercial washing may not remove the parasite. Sources: FDA outbreak investigation (updated 9/11/2026); FDA Cyclospora page.
