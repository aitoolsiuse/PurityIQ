Notes
- Not confirmed from a primary source and therefore left out: whether the West Virginia law has been enjoined by a court (only law-firm and news leads seen); whether Red No. 3 has been removed from the CFR text (the 2024 CFR still lists it at 74.303, and the 2025 revocation order was seen only via the FDA page, not the Federal Register text); any US FDA mandatory ban or warning for Red 40, Yellow 5, Yellow 6 as of today; whether industry pledges have been met.
- The Annex V table read is from the as-adopted 2008 text; the consolidated page (23/04/2025) confirmed the regulation is in force and Art. 24(1) but its excerpt did not display Annex V. Annex V was rechecked in the consolidated 23.04.2025 text (see row above); the list and wording match.
- EU names E 122 "Carmoisine" in Annex V; EFSA also calls it azorubine. E 129 is Allura red AC (Annex V "Allura red").
- The EU wording says "may have an adverse effect on activity and attention in children". EFSA's own 2009 statement found no causal link for individual colours. Present both, name the bodies, avoid implying harm beyond that. Frame as divergence, not "stricter is better".
- 21 CFR listing text says "may be safely used"; that phrase is a verbatim quote from the CFR and should not be reproduced on slides. The West Virginia statute and HHS release contain other words on our banned list; do not quote them.
- eCFR and Federal Register were not used (redirect to unverified host risk); CFR text from govinfo 2024 edition. The FDA Red No. 3 and phase-out pages are the currency checks; FDA pages fetched carry dates 01/15/2025, 22 Apr 2025, 5 Feb 2026 and 07/22/2026.
- The 22 Apr 2025 "end of 2026" target is described by FDA as working with industry; it is not a regulation. As of 2026-09-26 the CFR 2024 listings for Red 40, Yellow 5, Yellow 6 remain (no revocation seen).
- Web page text was read through a summarizing fetch tool; quotes above should be spot-checked in a browser before appearing on a slide.
