title: Raw Milk and Your Kids

## Caption

FDA rules ban selling raw, unpasteurized milk across state lines for people to drink. CDC says raw milk can carry germs such as Listeria, E. coli and Salmonella, and that children under 5 and pregnant women are more likely to become seriously ill. Sources: 21 CFR 1240.61 (FDA); CDC, Raw Milk (updated January 31, 2025).
