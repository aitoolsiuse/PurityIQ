## Caption

A new California law bars selling food that contains potassium bromate starting January 1, 2027. The EU's list of authorized food additives does not include it, while US standards of identity still permit it in flour and bread. Sources: California AB 418 (Chapter 328, Statutes of 2023, codified at section 109025); Regulation (EC) No 1333/2008, consolidated version of 18 August 2026; 21 CFR 137.155 and 136.110; OEHHA Proposition 65 list; FDA List of Select Chemicals in the Food Supply Under FDA Review.
