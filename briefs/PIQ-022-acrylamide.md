title: Acrylamide in Fried and Roasted Foods

## Caption

Acrylamide forms when starchy foods are fried, baked, or roasted -- chips, fries, toast, coffee. California excused coffee's roasting-created acrylamide from a cancer warning in 2019, but other acrylamide foods still carry one. FDA has no limit, only voluntary guidance. Sources: FDA, Cal. Code Regs. tit. 27 section 25704.
