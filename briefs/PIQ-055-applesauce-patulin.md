title: Applesauce Patulin Recall

## Caption

An organic unsweetened applesauce was recalled after routine FDA testing found elevated patulin, a compound made by molds on damaged or bruised apples. FDA's patulin guideline is written for apple juice (50 parts per billion); the EU also limits solid apple products, with a lower limit for foods for infants and young children. Check the recall notice for UPC and best-by date. Sources: FDA-posted recall notice (Sept 2026); FDA CPG 510.150; Regulation (EU) 2023/915.
