title: Arsenic in Apple Juice

## Caption

In June 2023 FDA finalized an action level of 10 parts per billion for inorganic arsenic in apple juice. It is guidance: FDA says its guidances 'should be viewed only as recommendations.' FDA chose the level to account for children who drink the most apple juice, estimating a 42 percent cut in their exposure. Source: FDA, Action Level for Inorganic Arsenic in Apple Juice: Guidance for Industry (June 2023).
