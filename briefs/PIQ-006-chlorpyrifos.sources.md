Notes
- CORRECTION to the brief request: Regulation (EU) 2023/334 does not concern chlorpyrifos. EUR-Lex shows it amends MRLs for clothianidin and thiamethoxam (application date 7 Mar 2026 belongs to that regulation). The chlorpyrifos and chlorpyrifos-methyl MRL regulation is (EU) 2020/1085. Do not cite 2023/334 or a 2026-03-07 date for chlorpyrifos. (2023/377 also checked: it does not cover chlorpyrifos.)
- CORRECTION on US status: the December 2024 EPA action is a proposed rule, not a final rule. No final rule limiting tolerances to 11 crops is found as of 2026-09-24 (eCFR unchanged since 5 Feb 2024; EPA page updated 24 Aug 2026 still says tolerances remain until a final rule is issued). What did become final is the cancellation of most food-use registrations (use ban on cancelled crops from 1 Jul 2025), which is a FIFRA use rule, not a tolerance change.
- Date oddity to check before any slide uses it: 2020/1085 Article 2 as printed in the EN HTML, FR HTML and EN PDF says it "shall apply from 6 August 2020", which is before its entry into force (20th day after 24.7.2020 publication). Recital 8 mentions a reasonable period before the MRLs become applicable. Use only "2020" on slides unless a human resolves this.
- The EU MRL for chlorpyrifos is now a default limit of determination of 0.01 mg/kg (Annex V), not a ban on the substance in imported food as a categorical statement. Frame as divergence: the EU no longer approves it and set 0.01 mg/kg default; the US still lists tolerances.
- "Applies to imports" is read from Reg. 396/2005 Art. 2 and 18(1) (no origin exemption; applies to products placed on the market). No separate EU statement about imported food specific to chlorpyrifos was found in 2020/1085 text read.
- PDP figures are "detected" (lab-tested residue at or above the method limit of detection), not "contains" on a label. PDP samples are a national survey set of specific commodities in 2024 only; they do not describe every product of that type. Origin (domestic vs imported) for chlorpyrifos detections is not in the summary text read; it would need the PDP database.
- PDP tolerance column marks most chlorpyrifos rows "FH"; the legend for that code was not located in the text read, so it is not interpreted here. (It matches the 0.1 ppm food-service-establishment tolerance in 180.342(a)(3), but that link is unconfirmed.)
- Not confirmed: any health effect statement beyond what EFSA is quoted as saying in the EU regulations; EPA's current health finding (EPA page shows 2020 assessment history only in the sections read); a 2025 PDP summary (none listed as of today); anything about labels (no label rule or source was consulted; nothing here supports a claim that chlorpyrifos appears on a food label).
- eCFR requests used the eCFR API directly (ecfr.gov, no redirect to the unverified host). federalregister.gov was not used.
