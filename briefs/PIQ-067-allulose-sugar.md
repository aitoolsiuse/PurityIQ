title: Is Allulose a Sugar?

## Caption

FDA lets companies leave the sweetener allulose out of the Total Sugars and Added Sugars lines (guidance, October 2020). On July 27, 2026, a federal appeals court ruled that allulose is a sugar under FDA's sugar-free rule and let a 'zero sugar' labeling suit proceed. The ruling applies in Illinois, Indiana and Wisconsin; the case returns to district court. Sources: FDA guidance on allulose labeling; US Court of Appeals for the Seventh Circuit opinion (7/27/2026).
