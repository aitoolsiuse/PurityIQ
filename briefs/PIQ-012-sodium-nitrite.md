title: Sodium Nitrite

## Caption

The EU lowered its allowed limits for sodium nitrite and nitrate in cured meats, effective October 9, 2025. The US limit of 200 parts per million has not changed. Both bodies act because nitrites and nitrates can form nitrosamines, some of which the World Health Organization classifies as carcinogenic. Sources: Commission Regulation (EU) 2023/2108; 9 CFR 424.21 and 317.17; 21 CFR 172.175; IARC Press Release No. 240 (October 26, 2015). Weekly breakdown at the link in bio.
