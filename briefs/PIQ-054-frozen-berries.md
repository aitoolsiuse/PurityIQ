## Caption

FDA linked organic frozen blueberries from a Chilean grower to 17 E. coli O145 cases and added the grower to an import alert; recalled product has best-by dates into 2028. In Canada, CFIA recalled a frozen raspberry lot for norovirus after reported illnesses. Check recall notices for lot codes. Sources: FDA outbreak investigation (updated 9/21/2026); FDA recall notices; CFIA recall (9/15/2026).
