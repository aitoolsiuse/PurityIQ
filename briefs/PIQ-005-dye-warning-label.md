## Caption

The EU puts a warning on foods with six synthetic dyes. The US does not require one. Europe's food safety agency found no proven link for the individual dyes, and FDA says most children have no adverse effects. Sources: Regulation (EC) No 1333/2008 Annex V and Article 24; EFSA news release of 12 November 2009; 21 CFR 74.340, 74.705, 74.706 and 101.22(k); FDA Color Additives, Information for Consumers.
