## Caption

The color in dark sodas can contain 4-MEI, a compound California lists as a carcinogen (OEHHA, Prop 65). California requires a warning above 29 micrograms a day of exposure; FDA has reviewed the same chemical and set no federal limit or labeling rule. Sources: OEHHA, FDA.
