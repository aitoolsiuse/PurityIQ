## Caption

In USDA's 2024 testing, 87.6% of 531 almond samples had at least one detectable pesticide residue. None exceeded an EPA tolerance. Five samples had a residue with no EPA tolerance set for almonds at all. Detected does not mean over the limit. Sources: USDA AMS Pesticide Data Program Annual Summary, Calendar Year 2024 (December 2025), Appendix C and the Executive Summary. Weekly breakdown at the link in bio.
