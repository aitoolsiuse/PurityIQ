title: How Imported Mangoes Get Tested

## Caption

FDA sampling found Salmonella in mangoes grown on a Mexican farm, before they reached US stores. The importer then recalled 302 boxes of mangoes from the same farm, sold at select Walmart stores in CT, NY, NJ and PA in August 2026, identified by PLU 4584. No illnesses reported. Source: FDA-posted recall (8/28/2026).
