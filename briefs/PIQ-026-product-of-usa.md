title: What Product of USA Now Means

## Caption

Until 2026, USDA let meat that was imported and then minimally processed in the US carry a 'Product of USA' label. Under USDA's final rule (March 2024, compliance January 1, 2026), the claim now requires animals born, raised, slaughtered and processed in the United States. The claim stays voluntary. Source: USDA FSIS final rule, Voluntary Labeling of FSIS-Regulated Products With U.S.-Origin Claims (Federal Register, March 18, 2024).
