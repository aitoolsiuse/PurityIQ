title: FDA's Infant Formula Testing

## Caption

FDA tested more than 300 infant formula samples sold in the US for lead, mercury, cadmium, arsenic, pesticides, PFAS and phthalates (results April 29, 2026). Glyphosate and glufosinate were not detected in any sample and 99 percent had no detectable pesticides. FDA says it is working to establish action levels for contaminants in infant formula; baby food already has FDA lead action levels. Sources: FDA press release and testing results (April 29, 2026); FDA, Infant Formulas Marketed in the U.S.
