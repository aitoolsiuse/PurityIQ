title: Requeson Cheese Listeria

## Caption

FDA reports a Listeria outbreak linked to requeson, a soft ricotta-style cheese: 15 people in 4 states, 14 hospitalized, 1 death (final update 8/26/2026). The cheese was relabeled under several brand names. FDA advises people who are pregnant, 65 or older, or have weakened immune systems not to eat any queso fresco-type cheese, including requeson. Source: FDA outbreak investigation, Listeria, soft cheese (June 2026).
