title: Neonicotinoid Pesticides

## Caption

Three neonicotinoid pesticides now have no approved use left in the EU, not even in greenhouses. The US still allows them on food crops. For imidacloprid on apples, the EU's import limit is 0.01 mg/kg; the US tolerance is 0.5 ppm, a 50 times gap on the same fruit. USDA's own testing is public and searchable by food. Sources: Commission Implementing Regulations (EU) 2018/783, 2018/784 and 2018/785; 40 CFR 180 (imidacloprid, clothianidin); USDA AMS Pesticide Data Program Annual Summary, Calendar Year 2024.
