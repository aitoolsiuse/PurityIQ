title: Two Sprout Outbreaks

## Caption

FDA is tracking two sprout outbreaks: broccoli sprouts linked to 32 Salmonella cases in six states, and alfalfa sprouts linked to 55 E. coli and Salmonella cases, traced partly to a recalled alfalfa seed lot sold for home sprouting. FDA advises children, older adults, pregnant women and people with weakened immune systems to avoid raw or lightly cooked sprouts. Sources: FDA outbreak investigations (updated Sept 24, 2026); FDA, Selecting and Serving Produce Safely.
