title: Added Sugar on Toddler Labels

## Caption

FDA's Nutrition Facts rule sets an added-sugars Daily Value of 25 grams for children 1 through 3. The 2025-2030 Dietary Guidelines (USDA and HHS) say to avoid added sugars in infancy and early childhood. Sources: 21 CFR 101.9; Dietary Guidelines for Americans 2025-2030.
