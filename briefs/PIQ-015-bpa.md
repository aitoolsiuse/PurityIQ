## Caption

As of January 2025, the EU has banned BPA from nearly all food packaging, including can coatings, after cutting its own daily intake limit 20,000-fold. FDA's current position, unchanged since 2023, is that BPA is acceptable at the levels found in food. A 2022 petition asking FDA to tighten its limit is still under review. Sources: Commission Regulation (EU) 2024/3190; FDA, Bisphenol A (BPA), Use in Food Contact Application; FDA docket FDA-2022-F-1108; California Proposition 65 list, OEHHA.
