title: Percent Juice on Fruit Drinks

## Caption

Any drink that pictures fruit or implies juice must state its percent juice near the top of the information panel, down to 'less than 1 percent juice.' CDC says children should not have fruit drinks, which contain added sugars. Sources: 21 CFR 101.30; CDC, Foods and Drinks to Avoid or Limit (infant and toddler nutrition).
