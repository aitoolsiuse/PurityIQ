title: Growth Hormones in Beef

## Caption

The EU has banned one hormone used to grow US beef cattle, estradiol, outright since the 1980s, with no exception. FDA approves it and four others as growth implants. For two of the five, trenbolone and zeranol, FDA doesn't require a tissue residue limit at all. Sources: Council Directive 96/22/EC; 21 CFR 522.840 and 522.2477; 21 CFR 556.739 and 556.760; USDA FSIS labeling glossary. Weekly breakdown at the link in bio.
