title: Raw Breaded Stuffed Chicken

## Caption

USDA declared raw breaded stuffed chicken with Salmonella at 1 CFU per gram or more adulterated, effective May 1, 2025. In December 2025 USDA delayed testing again and set no new start date. USDA says these products are under 0.15 percent of the chicken supply but about 5 percent of chicken-associated Salmonella outbreaks. Sources: USDA FSIS, 89 FR 35033 (May 1, 2024); FSIS notice FR Doc. 2025-21737.
