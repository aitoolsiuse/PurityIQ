title: What 80% Lean Really Means

## Caption

USDA rules: the claim 'lean' on meat means under 10 grams of fat per 100 grams. A '% lean' statement like '80% lean' is allowed only with the fat percentage right next to it, same size and color. Ground beef can be up to 30 percent fat and can't have beef fat added; 'hamburger' can have beef fat added. Sources: 9 CFR 317.362; 9 CFR 319.15.
