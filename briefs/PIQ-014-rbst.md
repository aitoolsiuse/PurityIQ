## Caption

The EU has prohibited using rBST, a synthetic growth hormone, in dairy cows since 2000. FDA approved it for US dairy cows in 1993 and says milk composition doesn't measurably differ either way. A carton can say "from cows not treated with rbST," but by FDA rule it must also carry a specific disclaimer sentence. Sources: FDA, Bovine Somatotropin (bST); FDA Freedom of Information Summary, Supplemental NADA 140-872; Federal Register, February 10, 1994; Council Decision 1999/879/EC; Health Canada, Hormonal Growth Promoters.
