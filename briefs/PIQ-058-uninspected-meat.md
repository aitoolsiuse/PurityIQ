## Caption

In September 2026 USDA issued Class I recalls for a ready-to-eat chicken stew imported from Korea without import reinspection, snack mixes with pork cracklings from Colombia deemed ineligible, and 167,639 pounds of meat sold with a false USDA mark of inspection. Check recall notices at fsis.usda.gov. Sources: USDA FSIS recall notices (Sept 9-25, 2026).
