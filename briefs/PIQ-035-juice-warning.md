title: Unpasteurized Juice Warnings

## Caption

FDA requires a boxed warning on packaged juice that hasn't been pasteurized or treated to cut germs 100,000-fold. FDA does not require that warning on juice or cider sold by the glass, such as at orchards, farmers' markets and juice bars. Sources: 21 CFR 101.17(g); FDA, What You Need to Know About Juice Safety.
