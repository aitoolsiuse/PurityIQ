## Caption

The EU stopped approving the pesticide chlorpyrifos in 2020. EPA still lists US tolerances for it on dozens of food and feed items. In USDA's 2024 testing it was detected in 36 of 9,165 fruit and vegetable samples. Detected does not mean over the limit. Sources: Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/18 and Regulation (EU) 2020/1085; 40 CFR 180.342; EPA chlorpyrifos page (updated August 24, 2026); USDA AMS Pesticide Data Program Annual Summary, Calendar Year 2024, Appendix B.
