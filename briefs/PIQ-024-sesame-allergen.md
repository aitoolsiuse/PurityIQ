title: Sesame Added on Purpose

## Caption

Sesame became the 9th major food allergen on January 1, 2023, under the FASTER Act. FDA says some manufacturers responded by intentionally adding sesame to products and labeling it, which keeps them compliant but limits options for people allergic to sesame. Sources: FDA, Food Allergies; FDA Voices, An Update on Sesame Allergen Labeling on Food Packages (July 27, 2023).
