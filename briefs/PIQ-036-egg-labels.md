title: Egg Carton Handling Labels

## Caption

FDA requires egg cartons to carry handling instructions, and stores to keep eggs at 45 degrees F or colder. Eggs treated to destroy Salmonella, such as in-shell pasteurized eggs, are exempt. FDA says to use treated eggs for dishes served raw or runny. Sources: 21 CFR 101.17(h); 21 CFR 115.50; FDA, What You Need to Know About Egg Safety.
