Notes
- Fetch methods: FSIS, EUR-Lex, USDA and AMS pages return 403 or a bot challenge to command-line and WebFetch requests. They were read in the browser (Claude in Chrome) as rendered pages; the FSIS xlsx datasets and PDFs were parsed in the browser. eCFR (ecfr.gov versioner API) and federalregister.gov API were fetched directly; no redirect to unblock.federalregister.gov occurred or was followed. CFR editions from govinfo.gov were fetched as in PIQ-002.
- The FSIS datasets list only results with a classification of "Detected, Non-Violative" or "Violative" (about 250 to 370 rows per quarter across all analytes). They give no count of samples tested with no ractopamine found, so no percentage can be derived. Say "detected", never "contains".
- Codex versus US numbers: Codex MRLs are in ug/kg and US tolerances in ppm (1 ppm = 1,000 ug/kg). The numbers differ (for example pig muscle 10 ug/kg Codex vs 0.05 ppm = 50 ug/kg US tolerance; pig liver 40 ug/kg vs 0.15 ppm = 150 ug/kg). Whether the two limits are expressed for the same marker residue/compound was not verified, so a direct like-for-like comparison is not confirmed. Present as "different numbers", not "US is looser".
- The EU sources name the class "beta-agonists", not ractopamine. FSIS's own residue list classifies ractopamine as a beta-agonist, which is the link between the two. No Commission page naming ractopamine was retrievable.
- 21 CFR 558.500 (2026 text) contains no withdrawal-time statement. The eCFR text as of 2026-09-24 was read in full.
- FSIS-GD-2024-0006 (Substantiating Animal-Raising or Environment-Related Labeling Claims, August 2024) has no mention of "ractopamine" or "beta agonist" (searched full text). It sets documentation expectations for negative hormone and antibiotic claims only. So there is no FSIS-defined meaning of "Never fed beta-agonists" in that guideline; the meaning in a given product is set by the specific program or claim documentation.
- Do not name brands. The Federal Register notices and the AMS listing contain company and product names; none are used here.
