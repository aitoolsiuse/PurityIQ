title: Pistachio Salmonella

## Caption

Canada closed a Salmonella outbreak linked to pistachios from Iran and pistachio products at 200 lab-confirmed cases, 26 hospitalized (July 21, 2026). Canada advises checking the country of origin. In the US, a pistachio nut butter sold at Walmart was recalled after Florida inspectors found Salmonella in jars on a store shelf (FDA-posted 8/3/2026). Sources: Public Health Agency of Canada; Canadian Food Inspection Agency; FDA-posted recall.
