title: Heavy Metal Testing in Baby Food

## Caption

Since 2024, California has required baby food makers to test each batch at least monthly for lead, arsenic, cadmium and mercury. Since 2025, they must post the results online and put a QR code on the label. FDA's lead levels for baby food, issued January 2025, are recommendations, not binding rules. Sources: California Assembly Bill 899 (Health and Safety Code 110962-110963); FDA, Action Levels for Lead in Processed Food Intended for Babies and Young Children (January 2025).
