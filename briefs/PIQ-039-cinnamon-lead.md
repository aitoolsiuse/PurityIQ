title: Lead in Ground Cinnamon

## Caption

After the October 2023 recall of cinnamon applesauce pouches with elevated lead, FDA tested ground cinnamon and keeps adding products to a public health alert, most recently on September 28, 2026. FDA says to throw away listed products and check lot codes on the label. Sources: FDA alerts on ground cinnamon (2024, updated 09/28/2026); FDA statement, March 6, 2024; FDA post-incident response on cinnamon applesauce pouches.
