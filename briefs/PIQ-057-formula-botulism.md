## Caption

FDA reports four infants hospitalized with botulism in an outbreak linked to a powdered whole milk infant formula (outbreak over, 8/27/2026). FDA says the linked lots used the same milk and spray-drying suppliers as the 2025 infant botulism outbreak, and evidence indicates a common source. Recalled cans have best-by dates into 2027; the lot code is on the bottom of the can. Sources: FDA outbreak investigation (8/27/2026); FDA-posted recall (6/13/2026).
