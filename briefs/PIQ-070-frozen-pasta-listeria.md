## Caption

Two lots of a frozen store-brand lemon alfredo fettuccine sold nationwide at Walmart were recalled for possible Listeria, after routine sampling by Washington and Florida state agriculture departments (FDA-posted 9/15/2026). No illnesses reported. Check UPC 194346442706, lots L6079C or L6080C, expiration 09/19/2027 or 09/20/2027. Source: FDA-posted recall notice.
