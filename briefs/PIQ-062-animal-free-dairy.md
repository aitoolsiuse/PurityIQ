title: Animal-Free Dairy Labels

## Caption

Some foods use milk proteins made by microbes through fermentation. FDA had no questions about one such protein (beta-lactoglobulin) in 2020 and says it must be labeled as a milk allergen. Since June 8, 2026, Louisiana requires the front-label statement 'Produced through microbial fermentation. Not derived from mammalian lactation.' in at least 30-point type. Sources: Louisiana Act 837 (HB 783, 2026); FDA GRAS Notice 863; FDA letter on beta-lactoglobulin labeling.
