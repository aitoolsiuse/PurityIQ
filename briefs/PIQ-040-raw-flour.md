title: Raw Flour and Cookie Dough

## Caption

FDA says flour is a raw food: processing grain into flour does not kill bacteria. Outbreaks since 2009 tied to raw flour and flour products caused 168 known illnesses and 20 hospitalizations. FDA says don't eat raw dough or let kids play with it, and don't try to heat-treat flour at home. Sources: FDA, Handling Flour Safely; FDA consumer update on raw flour.
