title: EU Limits PFAS in Food Packaging

## Caption

From August 12, 2026, the EU bars food-contact packaging with PFAS above set limits. The European Commission names takeaway containers, fast-food wrappers, microwave popcorn bags, bakery paper and pizza boxes as past uses. FDA says PFAS grease-proofing agents for paper food packaging are no longer sold into the US market, but the US has no equivalent packaging limit. Sources: Regulation (EU) 2025/40 (Art. 5); European Commission (Aug 11, 2026); FDA, Authorized uses of PFAS in food contact applications.
