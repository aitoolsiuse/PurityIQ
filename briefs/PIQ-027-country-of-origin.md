title: Chicken Shows Its Origin, Steak Doesn't

## Caption

USDA's mandatory country of origin labeling covers muscle cuts and ground chicken, lamb and goat, fresh produce, peanuts, macadamia nuts, pecans and ginseng. Beef and pork are not on the list, and covered foods lose the requirement once they become an ingredient in a processed food. Source: 7 CFR 65.135 and 65.300.
