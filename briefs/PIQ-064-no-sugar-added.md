title: No Sugar Added vs Sugar Free

## Caption

FDA rules: 'sugar free' means less than 0.5 grams of sugars per serving. 'No sugar added' means no sugars or sugar-containing ingredients like concentrated fruit juice were added during processing, and the label must direct you to the nutrition panel. The food can still contain naturally occurring sugar. Source: 21 CFR 101.60(c).
