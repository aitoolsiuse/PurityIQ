Notes
- STORY-FIT CAVEAT (same shape as PIQ-006): a parent cannot check imidacloprid or clothianidin status in the aisle from a label. The actionable angle is the same as PIQ-010 - PDP's database is public and searchable by commodity - not a label check.
- Three separate substances are named in the brief (imidacloprid, clothianidin, thiamethoxam). Thiamethoxam data were also pulled for context (Appendix B, "Thiamethoxam (insecticide) (also a parent of Clothianidin)," TOTAL 231/9,165; e.g., Cucumbers 130/708 (18.4%, range 0.003–0.28 ppm, tol. 0.2), Potatoes 17/177 (9.6%)) but the brief's US section asked specifically about imidacloprid and clothianidin, so thiamethoxam PDP/CFR figures are not included as full claims above - flag to the writer if a thiamethoxam-specific slide is wanted, since the eCFR section (40 CFR 180.622) was not separately pulled.
- Discrepancy not fully resolved: PDP's Appendix B lists the imidacloprid tolerance for "Apples" as 0.6 ppm, but the current eCFR text has a specific "Apple" line at 0.5 ppm and a broader "Fruit, pome, group 11" line at 0.6 ppm. PDP may be citing the group tolerance rather than the specific commodity tolerance, or the specific line may postdate PDP's tolerance references ("EPA tolerances cited in this summary and appendixes apply to 2024 and not to the current year" - PDP's own caveat). Use 0.6 ppm only if citing PDP directly as the source of that number; use 0.5 ppm if citing 40 CFR 180.472 directly for "Apple."
- CORRECTION carried over from PIQ-006: Commission Regulation (EU) 2023/334 does NOT concern chlorpyrifos; it concerns clothianidin and thiamethoxam MRLs, applies from 7 March 2026, and is confirmed above as fully in force as of today (2026-09-27).
- The CJEU judgment text itself (Case C-162/21) could not be retrieved directly from curia.europa.eu/InfoCuria in this session (page navigation returned a 404 on the docid tried); the ruling and its date are instead sourced from the European Commission's own "Neonicotinoids" page, which is an EU Commission source, not news, and quotes the case number and date directly.
