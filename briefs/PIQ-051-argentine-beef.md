title: Argentine Beef Recall

## Caption

USDA recalled 29,628 pounds of raw beef imported from Argentina without import reinspection, sold to distributors and retailers in Florida and Texas (Class I, Aug 7, 2026; no confirmed illnesses). Separately, a February 2026 proclamation raised Argentina's lean beef quota by 80,000 metric tons; imported lean trimmings are blended into ground beef. Since Jan 1, 2026, 'Product of USA' on beef means born, raised, slaughtered and processed in the US. Sources: USDA FSIS recall notice; Proclamations 11010 and 11059 (Federal Register); 9 CFR 412.3.
