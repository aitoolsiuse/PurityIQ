## Caption

The EU prohibits beta-agonists in farm animals. FDA approves one of them, ractopamine, in feed for finishing pigs and cattle. Since April 2026, FDA requires feed labels to note that behavioral signs such as hyperactivity, anxiety and aggression have been reported in pigs fed ractopamine. That is about the animals, not about people. Detected does not mean over the limit: every ractopamine row in USDA FSIS residue data is classed non-violative. Sources: Directive 96/22/EC; Delegated Regulation (EU) 2022/2292, Article 10; 21 CFR 558.500 and 556.570; FDA final rule, 91 FR 20337 (April 16, 2026); USDA FSIS residue datasets, FY2025 to FY2026; USDA AMS Official Listing of Process Verified Programs (September 18, 2026).
