title: Moringa Powder Salmonella

## Caption

FDA linked moringa leaf powder imported from India to three Salmonella outbreaks in 2026; the largest reached 131 people in 38 states, 36 hospitalized. In one outbreak, a breastfeeding mother's infant was sick with the outbreak strain. FDA increased screening of imported moringa powder, and recalls of moringa products continued through September 4, 2026. Brands and lot codes are in FDA's recall notices. Sources: FDA outbreak investigations (Jan, Feb, May 2026); FDA recall notices (Aug-Sep 2026).
