title: Egg Salmonella in the US and UK

## Caption

In the US, 134 people in 18 states got Salmonella from recalled eggs; recalled cartons were identified by a plant code printed on the side (outbreak over, FDA 9/3/2026). In the UK, health officials say 586 Salmonella cases in three clusters may be linked to imported eggs, and advise vulnerable people, including young children, to avoid raw or lightly cooked egg dishes when eating out. Sources: FDA outbreak investigation; UK Health Security Agency / Food Standards Agency update (Sept 17, 2026).
