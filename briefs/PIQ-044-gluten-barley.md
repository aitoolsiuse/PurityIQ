title: Barley Hiding on Labels

## Caption

Wheat is a major allergen that must be declared, but barley and rye are not, even though they contain gluten. FDA notes barley can appear as 'malt extract' or 'malt syrup,' and opened a request for information on gluten labeling in January 2026. A 'gluten-free' claim means under 20 parts per million gluten. Sources: FDA, Gluten and Food Labeling; 91 FR 2781 (Jan 22, 2026); 21 CFR 101.91.
