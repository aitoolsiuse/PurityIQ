title: Raw Milk Cheese E. coli Outbreak

## Caption

FDA and CDC are investigating E. coli O26:H11 infections linked to a grass-fed raw milk cheese: 13 sick in 9 states, 8 hospitalized, 3 with hemolytic uremic syndrome (a kidney complication). All 9 people interviewed ate the cheese, and the company recalled the whole product line. Check the recall notice for product names and UPCs. Sources: FDA outbreak investigation (Sept 25, 2026); FDA recall notice (Sept 29, 2026).
