title: Toddler Pouch Recall

## Caption

A toddler fruit puree pouch sold at Kroger, Meijer and Target between March 6 and July 13, 2026 was recalled because an internal packaging defect may leave a strand of soft plastic in the product. No injuries were reported. Check the barcode, the number 4 on the rear right seam, and the best-before dates listed in FDA's recall notice. Source: FDA-posted recall (7/21/2026).
