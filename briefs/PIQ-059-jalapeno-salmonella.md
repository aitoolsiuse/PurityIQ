title: Jalapeno Salmonella Outbreak

## Caption

FDA reported 431 people with Salmonella in 32 states and 57 hospitalizations linked to fresh jalapenos from Sinaloa, Mexico (FDA page current as of 8/21/2026, outbreak listed ongoing). Most ate at Mexican-style restaurants; some manufacturers used the peppers in products sold at retail, and USDA issued a public health alert for meat and poultry items containing them. Sources: FDA outbreak investigation; USDA FSIS public health alert (8/8/2026).
