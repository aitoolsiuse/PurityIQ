title: No Honey Before Age 1

## Caption

CDC says honey given to children younger than 12 months may cause botulism, and not to give honey or products made with it, including honey pacifiers, to infants. FDA counts honey as an added sugar. Sources: CDC, Foods and Drinks to Avoid or Limit; CDC, Botulism Prevention; 21 CFR 101.9.
