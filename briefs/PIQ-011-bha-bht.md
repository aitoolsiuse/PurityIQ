## Caption

The EU authorizes BHA and BHT only in industrial frying fat, lard and heat-treated cooking oil manufacturing, not in food you'd buy directly. The US permits both directly in dry breakfast cereal, chewing gum and other packaged foods. FDA opened a review of both in 2026. Sources: 21 CFR 172.110, 172.115, 182.3169, 182.3173; Commission Regulation (EU) No 1129/2011, Annex II; FDA dockets FDA-2026-N-0302 and FDA-2026-N-2526; Louisiana SB 14 (2025).
