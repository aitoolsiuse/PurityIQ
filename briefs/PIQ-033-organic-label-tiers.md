title: What Organic on the Label Means

## Caption

USDA organic labels come in tiers. '100 percent organic' means all organic ingredients. 'Organic' means at least 95 percent. 'Made with organic' needs at least 70 percent and cannot carry the USDA seal. Under 70 percent, organic ingredients can only be named in the ingredient list. Source: USDA National Organic Program, 7 CFR 205.301, 205.305, 205.311.
