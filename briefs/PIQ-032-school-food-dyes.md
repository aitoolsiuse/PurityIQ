title: Six Dyes Leaving California Schools

## Caption

California's AB 2316 (chaptered September 28, 2024) bars Red 40, Yellow 5, Yellow 6, Blue 1, Blue 2 and Green 3 from food and drinks sold or served in K-12 public schools starting December 31, 2027. FDA still lists Red 40 as a color that may be used in foods nationally. Sources: California AB 2316; 21 CFR 74.340.
