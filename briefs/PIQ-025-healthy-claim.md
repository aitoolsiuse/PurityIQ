## Caption

FDA updated what 'healthy' means on a food label. Under the final rule (December 2024), a food must contain a meaningful amount of a food group and stay under limits for added sugars, sodium and saturated fat. Salmon, nuts and olive oil now qualify; highly sweetened cereal and yogurt no longer do. Compliance date: February 25, 2028. Sources: FDA, Use of the Healthy Claim on Food Labeling; Federal Register, December 27, 2024.
